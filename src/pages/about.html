We built the trip planner we wished we had

Wanderly AI started as a shared spreadsheet for a friends' trip that got out of hand. Today it helps people turn a rough idea into a plan they trust.

Our mission

Good trips come from good decisions: where to stay, what to skip, when to rest. Most planners hand you a list of everything and leave the deciding to you.

Wanderly AI makes those decisions with you. It asks about your budget and pace, drafts a plan, and explains why each stop is there, so you can change what doesn't fit.

How we got here

  1. March 2024

    The spreadsheet

    Eight friends, one Lisbon trip and a spreadsheet with 14 tabs. We wrote a script to sort it out, and it worked.

  2. January 2025

    First prototype

    We added a language model that turns a sentence into a draft itinerary. Fifty friends and family tested it.

  3. September 2025

    Public beta

    Budgets, pacing and group voting shipped. The first 10,000 trips were planned in six weeks.

  4. 2026

    Offline plans and live prices

    Itineraries now work without signal, and every stop shows a checked price and opening time.

What goes into a plan

Only four things: where, how long, how much you want to spend and what you enjoy. Everything else is decided from those and your edits.

Opening hours and prices come from public listings and local partners. Our team reviews popular destinations every month.

It won't book anything for you, hide sponsored places inside your plan or promise a price it hasn't checked.
